# Prototype privacy notice
Tour Memory stores preferences, property notes, contacts and drafts in browser local storage. Clear local tour memory removes these records and cached listings and resets preferences to their defaults. Exports and messages opened in other apps are not deleted by this action. With configured services, questions, relevant notes and property context may be sent to the configured Claude or OpenAI provider; short microphone recordings are sent for transcription. Confirmed Gmail draft requests go to Gmail. Listing requests go to RentCast when enabled. Do not enter sensitive financial or identity documents into this prototype. No automatic message sending. This is a local single-user prototype, not a production privacy policy. Production deployment requires authentication, retention controls and review of applicable privacy and fair housing requirements.

Recent conversation history and property notes are sent to the configured AI provider when AI is enabled; clearing local memory removes local history.

V8: location is requested on demand, not tracked in the background. Live nearby searches transmit coordinates to RentCast via the server. Phone speech may be processed by the browser vendor; G2 audio may be processed by Deepgram or OpenAI depending on configuration.

V9: explicit neighborhood lookup sends selected listing coordinates to the public Overpass service; results are session-only. Official source links open external sites. No personal registry records are collected or used in scoring.

Opt-in alert history and price baselines are stored locally and cleared with tour memory. Periodic listing checks occur only while visible; GPS remains on demand.

V11 stores self-reported communication status locally. Calendar exports are user initiated.

V12 household profiles, names, preferences, ratings and observations are stored locally in tour memory. Use consent before entering another person's information; exported reports contain these details and should be shared selectively. Clearing local memory removes household data. No household synchronization or authentication is provided.

Financial readiness inputs are processed in the browser and not saved by the calculator. Journal tasks are stored locally as before; avoid entering sensitive financial or identity details in notes.

When Vosk is selected, the G2 audio request is processed by the local server. The recognizer reads the recording from memory and returns a transcript. The app does not persist the audio. Saved notes and conversation history remain on the device.
